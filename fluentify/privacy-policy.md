# Fluentify Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2025-01-01_

Fluentify is designed to be privacy-first. We don’t run servers and we don’t track you.

## What data Fluentify processes
- **Your text**: The text you select or paste is sent **directly** from your browser to the AI provider you configure (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral) using your own API key. Fluentify does not proxy or store this data.
- **Local storage**: The extension stores your settings (theme, tone, provider, API keys) and a local history of up to **25** improved texts **in your browser storage only**.

## What Fluentify does **not** do
- No analytics.
- No cookies.
- No external logging.
- No transmission of data to servers controlled by the Fluentify developers.

## Third-party providers
When you use Fluentify, your requests are sent to the AI provider you choose:
- OpenAI (`api.openai.com`)
- Anthropic (`api.anthropic.com`)
- Mistral (`api.mistral.ai`)

Your use of these services is governed by each provider’s terms and privacy policy.

## Permissions (why we need them)
- `contextMenus` — to add right-click actions on selected text.
- `activeTab` / `scripting` — to read the current selection and replace it on the page.
- `storage` — to save your settings and local history.
- Host permissions — to call your chosen AI provider’s API.

## Security of your keys
API keys are saved in browser storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can delete them anytime from the Providers page or by removing the extension.

## Your choices
- Clear local history in **Settings → About**.
- Remove API keys or switch providers in **Settings → Providers**.
- Uninstall the extension at any time.

## Contact
Questions or issues? Open a GitHub issue or email the maintainer listed in the repository.


This privacy policy may be updated periodically. We'll notify users of any significant changes through:
- Website announcements
- Social media updates
- Direct communication if possible
