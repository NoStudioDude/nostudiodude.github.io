FAQ

Does it work everywhere?

Most sites and editors, yes. Browser-restricted pages (e.g., chrome://, web store, PDFs) don't allow content scripts.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes — go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

How are keys stored?

In your browser's local storage. You can remove them at any time.
