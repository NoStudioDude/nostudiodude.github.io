Kira Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Kira is designed as a local-first expense tracker. Your core expense data stays on your device. Some optional AI features may send limited information to process a specific request — never more than is needed.

1. Overview

Kira is designed as a local-first expense tracker. Core expense data is stored on your device. Some optional AI features may send limited information to process a specific request — they do not store your data permanently and are always optional.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device and does not leave your device as part of normal operation:

  • Expenses (amount, merchant, category, date, tags, notes)
  • Categories and custom tags
  • Settings and preferences
  • Onboarding selections
  • Usage counters (free plan limits)

3. AI features

Kira uses AI for optional features. These features are only activated when you choose to use them.

  • Voice input parsing: The transcript of your voice input is sent to process the expense entry.
  • Chat assistant: Your question and a summary of relevant spending data (not your full history) are sent to generate a response.
  • Receipt categorization: The extracted text from your receipt may be sent when needed for categorization assistance.

AI requests are stateless. Kira does not permanently store your data with any AI provider. Each request is independent.

4. Cloudflare proxy

Kira routes AI requests through a secure proxy so that the AI provider API key is not stored inside the app. Requests are processed solely to provide the requested feature. No user account is required for this proxy. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies to transit data processed through their infrastructure.

5. No bank connection

Kira does not require your bank login and does not connect directly to your bank account. No financial credentials are requested or stored by Kira.

6. Apple Pay Shortcut

If you use the Apple Pay Shortcut integration, payment details are sent to Kira only through the shortcut action you configure in iOS. Kira does not access Apple Wallet directly — this is an Apple restriction that applies to all third-party apps. The shortcut triggers a review flow in Kira, where you confirm before saving.

7. Subscriptions

Kira uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status and purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymized identifier to verify your subscription. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Kira does not store your payment information.

For RevenueCat's privacy practices, see revenuecat.com/privacy.

8. Data export and deletion

You are in control of your data:

  • Export: Open Kira → Settings → Data & Privacy → Export data (Premium feature).
  • Delete expenses: Open Kira → Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete all expenses.
  • Delete the app: Deleting the app from your device will remove locally stored data if it is not backed up to iCloud. Review your iCloud backup settings to manage this.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how Kira handles your data, please email nostudiodude@hotmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Kira after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
