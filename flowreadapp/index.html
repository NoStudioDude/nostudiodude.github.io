The problem

Your eyes drift.
Your mind wanders.

Traditional e-readers show too much text at once. Every toolbar, progress bar, and settings icon pulls your attention away from the words. For people with ADHD, dyslexia, or anyone in a high-stimulation environment, this is a constant fight — and most reading sessions end in re-scanning the same paragraph three times.

Losing the line

Your eye drifts, your place is gone. You re-read the last paragraph and still aren't sure where you were.

Too much on screen

Toolbars, chapter headers, percentage meters, settings icons — all competing with the actual words you're trying to read.

Speed you can't control

Traditional reading forces your pace to match your distractions. There's no friction forcing your eyes to stay on the word.

How it works

Three steps to flow state.

Import any book, tap to begin, and let the words come to you.

Step 01

Import your book

Drop in an EPUB, TXT, or Markdown file from iCloud Drive or your Files app. FlowRead parses it instantly — fully on-device.

Step 02

Tap to start

One tap begins the session. Words appear one at a time, dead center. Your eye stays perfectly still. No scanning, no drifting.

Step 03

Words flow at your pace

Swipe up to go faster, swipe down to slow down. Lost context? Swipe to see the surrounding paragraph. Your progress saves automatically.

Features

Built for deep focus.

Zero buttons

Every action is a gesture — tap, swipe, hold, drag. Nothing on screen but the word you're reading.

Three display modes

Large (1 word), Medium (3 words), or Small (5 words). Center word always highlighted.

Landscape layout

Runs exclusively in landscape — wider reading field, less vertical space, eye locked in one place.

Skim View

Swipe left for full paragraph context. Drag to scrub through text manually, then release to resume.

Auto-save progress

Your position per book is saved automatically. The last opened book relaunches on next open.

EPUB · TXT · MD

Import from iCloud Drive or local storage. Chapters, metadata, and cover art all extracted automatically.

Haptic feedback

Every gesture has a distinct tactile response — ramp pulse on start, dial-tick per WPM step, elastic double-pulse at limits.

Accessibility fonts

Atkinson Hyperlegible and Lexend bundled. Both designed for low-vision and dyslexic readers.

Fully offline

No account, no server, no sync. Books, progress, and settings live entirely on your device.

Gesture reference

No buttons. Just gestures.

Gesture Action
Tap anywhere Start / pause reading
Swipe up Increase reading speed (WPM)
Swipe down Decrease reading speed (WPM)
Swipe left Enter Context / Skim View
Drag in Skim View Scrub through text (left = back, right = forward)
Long press (paused) Open navigation menu
Swipe right (in menu) Close menu / return to reader
Privacy

Your books stay on your device.

No accounts. No tracking. No network requests — ever. All data is stored locally via SQLite and the iOS file system. Nothing leaves your phone.
