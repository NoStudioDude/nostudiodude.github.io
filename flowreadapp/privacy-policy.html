Privacy Policy

We collect nothing.

Last updated: 05/05/2026

FlowRead is built on one principle: your reading is your business. The app works entirely on your device. No server, no account, no data leaving your phone — ever.

What data FlowRead processes

None that leaves your device. The app stores your imported books, reading progress per book, and your settings locally on your device. No data is transmitted anywhere.

What FlowRead does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No cookies.
  • No user accounts or registration.
  • No network requests of any kind during normal operation.
  • No transmission of any data to any server controlled by the developer or any third party.
  • No crash reporting services.

Local storage

All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite and the iOS file system. The app stores:

  • Your imported book files (converted to an internal .rsvp format)
  • Reading progress (word index) per book
  • Your reading preferences and settings

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app from your device. There is no account to delete and no server-side data to request removal of, because none was ever created.

Third-party SDKs and libraries

FlowRead uses no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app uses only Apple system frameworks and the following open-source libraries, none of which collect, transmit, or report any data:

  • expo-haptics — haptic feedback
  • expo-sqlite — local database storage
  • expo-file-system — file import and management
  • react-native-gesture-handler — touch gesture recognition
  • react-native-reanimated — animations
  • JSZip — EPUB parsing (ZIP extraction)
  • fast-xml-parser — EPUB metadata parsing

Fonts

The app bundles Atkinson Hyperlegible and Lexend as static assets, both under the SIL Open Font License. No font loading services or CDNs are called. All fonts are included within the app bundle.

Encryption

FlowRead uses only standard iOS data storage APIs. The app's Info.plist declares ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption: false. No custom encryption is implemented.

Your choices

  • Delete individual books from within the app's library screen.
  • Delete all app data (books, progress, settings) by uninstalling the app from your device.
  • There is no account to delete, and no data stored outside your device to request removal of.

Children's privacy

FlowRead does not collect any information from anyone, including children. The app is a fully offline reading tool with no sign-up, no account, and no network access.
